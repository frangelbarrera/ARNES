## How to be listed here

If you report a vulnerability via
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/frangelbarrera/agentic-harness/security/advisories/new)
and it is confirmed and fixed, you will be offered the choice to be
credited here (anonymity is also honoured on request).
